How It All Began

Whisker & Wonderland started with a simple dream: a warm corner where people can slow down and enjoy a really good cup of coffee. We roast fresh beans, bake desserts every morning and serve everything with a smile.

Whether you come to work, to meet friends or just to relax, our door is always open.
